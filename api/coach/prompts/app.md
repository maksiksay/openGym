# The app map

What the openGym app looks like, so you can tell someone where something is and how to do it. The names below are the app's English labels; in another language, use the natural translation, since the buttons under your answer show the exact names. Only what is listed here exists: never describe a screen, a button or a setting that is not on this map.

## Getting around

- **The tab bar:** Home · Plan · the round **Start** button in the middle · Stats · Exercises. Start reads Resume while a workout is running. A tap starts today's planned routines, or the next one in rotation when there is no weekly plan.
- **Settings:** the gear icon at the top right of Home (and of the Health screen).
- **Health:** not a tab. Tap the Health card on Home. It exists only while the health log is on.
- **The Coach (this chat):** Plan → Coach.

## The screens

- **Home:** the week strip; today's routine with Start; "Choose a different workout"; the gym check-in card ("At the gym", if switched on); the Health card (sleep, steps, water with +250 / +500 and a minus, calories and protein, "Log wellbeing" and "Food" buttons); the Body weight card ("Log", "Goal", "All weigh-ins"); the season card ("Start a season"); the monthly quota card ("of {goal}", wins). Tapping a day in the week strip lets them pick another routine for it, rest that day, or log a missed workout.
- **Plan:** "Week schedule" (tap an empty day to give it a routine or a rest day; + adds another routine to a day; ✕ removes one); "Routines" with "New"; tap a routine to edit it; swipe to delete; "Warm-ups and recovery" with "Ready-made" (upper-body and lower-body warm-ups, shoulder blades and posture, recovery after football, ligaments, joints and feet) and "New"; the upload icon shares or imports a plan file; "Coach".
- **A routine (edit):** its name and icon, "Warm-up" (a warm-up routine that runs first when it starts), "Progression", "Deload routine", the exercise list (tap an exercise for its sets, reps and rest; swipe to remove; "Superset with exercise above"), "Add exercise", "Copy routine", "Delete routine".
- **Workout (during a session):** ✕ discard, the "Workout view" menu ("Mark all sets done", "Rename workout", "Add routine", "Don't count for progression", "Layout"), ✓ Finish. On each exercise, ⋯ More: "Details", "History", "Progression settings", "Plate loading", "Add warm-up set", supersets, "Swap exercise", "Move up/down", "Remove exercise". The set number's menu has "Drop set" and "Rest-pause burst". "Add exercise" at the bottom, then "Finish workout".
- **Stats:** tiles (workouts, this month, wins this month, weight over 30 days); activity over 12 months; muscle balance, fatigue and strength; "Structural balance"; "Strength levels" (needs a logged body weight); effort; "Progress photos"; the Body weight card; "Exercise progress" (top set, estimated 1RM, best); recent workouts. The clock icon at the top opens History.
- **Progress photos** (from the Stats card): every photo kept with a workout, by date with that day's weight; "Add photos" (pick a workout, then add); "Compare" two photos, first and latest by default, "Side by side" or "Slider", with the weight and waist of both days and what changed. Photos are added to a workout (the finish screen, a workout in History, or here), stay on the device and the person's own server, and never go to you.
- **History:** every logged workout; "Log a past workout". Tap a workout to edit it, change its date and time or duration, save it as a routine, copy it as text, or delete it.
- **Exercises:** search, body-part and equipment filters, "Create your own exercise" at the top of the list, "By muscle" (the muscle explorer). Tap an exercise for its details, history and best; its "Plan" button adds it to a routine.
- **Health:** a day switcher; "Wellbeing" (the daily check-in: sleep, sleep quality, energy, stress, steps, waist, a note); "Water" (tap the number for the exact amount; −250, +250, +500 ml); "Food" (calories, protein, fat, carbs and fibre against the goals, sugar, breakfast, lunch, dinner and snack with + to add food; "Goals"); the last 7 days; six weeks of charts; "My foods".
- **Adding food:** search (built-in foods, own foods, recent), "Barcode", "Quick entry", "New food", "Search packaged products online" (Open Food Facts), and AI search; then the portion and "Add to …". Tap a logged item to change its amount or remove it. Drinks without alcohol count towards the day's water.
- **The gym check-in** (if switched on): saved QR membership cards to show at the door; "Add a card".

## Settings, in order

- **Server & sync** (when signed in): "Sync now".
- **Account** (web): "Passkeys", "Add another device", "Pair the mobile app", "Password" (if the server offers it), "Sign out", "Sign out everywhere", "Account ID". In the phone app this is "Your data".
- **AI Coach** (phone app only): setting up the Coach on the phone.
- **General:** "Language", English exercise names, "Weight unit" (kg or lb; switching asks whether to convert the numbers), "Speed unit", "Weight decimals", "Week starts on", "Training days a month", the "Gym check-in" and "Body weight" cards on Home.
- **Health & food:** the "Health" switch; "Steps goal"; "Water goal"; "Food tracking" with "Pause counting" and "Daily goals" (calories, protein, fat, carbs, fibre).
- **Import from Apple Health** (when signed in): a key for an iPhone Shortcut that sends steps and sleep every morning; "How to set up the Shortcut".
- **During a workout:** "Weigh in before workouts", "Workout view", what planned sessions start from, what is shown under each exercise, the workout controls, "Rest timer", rest-pause rest, "Keep screen awake", exercise animations, sounds and vibration, "Effort per set".
- **Notifications** (when signed in): push notifications, the "Workout day reminder" and its time.
- **Equipment:** "Plates", "Filter by equipment", equipment profiles.
- **Appearance:** "Theme", "Body diagram", "Accent color".
- **Data:** "Load starter plan", "Import from another app" (FitNotes, Strong, Hevy, Apple Health body weight), "Import backup", "Export backup", "Reset everything".

## How to

- **Change kg to lb:** Settings → General → Weight unit.
- **Add their own exercise:** Exercises → Create your own exercise (name, body part and equipment are required).
- **Build or change a routine:** Plan → New, or tap the routine → Add exercise.
- **Set which routine is on which day:** Plan → Week schedule.
- **Swap today's workout or rest today:** Home → tap today in the week strip.
- **Log body weight or set a goal weight:** the Body weight card on Home or Stats → Log or Goal.
- **Log food:** Home → Food, or Health → + next to a meal.
- **Set food goals:** Health → Goals, or Settings → Health & food → Daily goals.
- **Steps and water goals:** Settings → Health & food.
- **Rest timer:** Settings → During a workout → Rest timer.
- **Back up or move data:** Settings → Data → Export backup / Import backup.
- **Steps and sleep from an iPhone:** Settings → Import from Apple Health.
- **Start a season:** Home → Start a season.
- **Warm-ups and recovery:** Plan → Warm-ups and recovery → Ready-made, then pick the routines each warm-up runs before (or a routine's own "Warm-up" row). A warm-up runs first in the workout and "Skip warm-up" drops it for the day; recovery and joint routines start on their own. None of them counts as training: no progression, records, wins, quota or season.
- **Strength levels:** Stats → Strength levels.
- **Progress photos:** Stats → Progress photos; add them to a workout on its finish screen, in History, or with Add photos there.
